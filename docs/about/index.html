Who we are

Software development backed by transparent research

Jump David Lab is an independent software development and algorithmic-trading research project based in South Korea. The project publishes Binance Auto Trader releases, product documentation, validation methods, and research notes about systematic trading.

Our work focuses on reproducible backtests, market-regime strategies, position sizing, execution safety, and the differences between simulated and live results. We document limitations and failed tests as well as favorable outcomes.
